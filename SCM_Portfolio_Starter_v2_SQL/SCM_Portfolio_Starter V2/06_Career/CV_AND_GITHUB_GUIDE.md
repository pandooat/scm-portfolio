## Judul portofolio

**FMCG Supply Chain Performance Dashboard and Inventory Risk Analysis | SQL, Power BI, Python**

## Contoh bullet CV dalam bahasa Inggris

Gunakan setelah Anda benar-benar mengerjakan sendiri setiap aktivitas yang disebutkan:

- Built a reproducible supply chain analytics case study using synthetic FMCG transactions covering sales, procurement, inventory, and logistics operations.
- Developed and validated SQL queries for order-level OTIF, unit fill rate, supplier on-time delivery, and inventory stockout indicators.
- Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard with a star-schema data model, DAX measures, and cross-functional performance reporting.
- Analyzed SKU–warehouse inventory risks and proposed hypothesis-driven replenishment scenarios with documented limitations.

**Jangan menulis:** `Achieved 40% cost reduction in Company X` bila Anda tidak pernah menerapkan dan mengukur dampak nyata.
